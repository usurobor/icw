## The theory returns verdicts, not only dissolutions

*Bears on: how the theory is described.*

The operation has four outcomes, and dissolution is one of them:

- **holds** — both are articulations; *which is really identity?* was malformed.
- **nested** — one continuity refines the other; the *both are equally
  articulations* answer is removed, and nothing else is.
- **untyped** — nothing is changing; the question does not arise.
- **uncarried** — undecided; the open item.

This corrects an earlier framing in which the theory was described as dissolving
debates generally. It does not. Most cases put to it will not return *holds*.

**Nested narrows; it does not adjudicate.** An earlier draft had it taking sides
— that a nested result in the personal-identity case puts the theory behind
animalism. It does not, for two reasons.

Microstate refines temperature, and nobody concludes from this that microstate
is the real identity and temperature a fiction. Both are genuine continuities.
Nesting privileges neither term, and a verdict that privileged one here would
have to privilege one there.

And the side supposedly defeated can welcome the verdict. A Lockean may say:
*yes, the psychological continuity refines the bodily one — and it is the
refinement that matters for personhood.* Nothing in the nested result contradicts
that. If the losing party can accept your finding intact, you were not
adjudicating.

What nested removes is the even-handed answer, that both are articulations of
one changing and the question of which is real was malformed. The rest of the
dispute survives untouched, because it turns on which continuity *matters*, and
mattering is not in this theory's jurisdiction.

This is the theory's service and its limit together: it relocates the argument
onto checkable ground — is the second direction attested, is the whole
well-typed — and then stops.

**What it does not establish.** Any verdict about a particular debate. Every
case named in discussion — personal identity, money, genotype and phenotype,
the linguistic sign — is a sketch. None has had the steps run.

## The verdict labels are under-resolved

*Bears on: the failure-mode taxonomy.*

Four labels currently cover about six distinguishable situations.

**untyped** spans: nothing changes (the portrait); logical sequence mistaken for
temporal change (a proof); and the two directions checked in different wholes.

**uncarried** spans: no ground at all (colour and shape over parked cars); and a
ground lying outside the changing (a rainbow, whose sameness cannot be fixed
without fixing a viewpoint).

If the theory's service is relocating arguments onto checkable ground, the
labels should track the checks one-to-one. They do not yet.

**Future work.** Split them, one label per check. Provisionally: *unchanging*
and *not-temporal* and *two-wholes* in place of untyped; *ungrounded* and
*grounded-outside* in place of uncarried. Naming is not the point; one check per
label is.

## Carrying cannot be another relation

*Bears on: O2 — what it is for one change to carry both continuities.*

Suppose carrying were a relation among occurrences. It would then sit alongside
the two continuities as a third relation, and we would immediately owe an
account of what makes *it* belong to the changing rather than being laid across
it. That regresses.

So carrying is a **predicate on relations** — a property a relation has with
respect to the changing — not a relation between things. This rules out the
most available formalization by construction.

A second form of the same argument, reached independently: suppose the whole's
own changing were admitted as a third term alongside the two continuities. Then
every continuity refines it and none cross-cuts it, so it cannot stand as a
peer. Whatever carrying is, it is not a further item in the series it grounds.

Two candidates have the right type: that the changing preserves the relation's
classes forward, and that the relation is generated by the changing rather than
imposed on it. Both are properties of a relation, not relations.

**Source.** Recovered from Holotheory 1.2.0, which distinguished *relationship
between parts* from *relationship to the whole*. The second is mis-typed there:
a part is not a thing standing apart from the whole, so it cannot be a relatum.
What it names is belonging, and belonging is what carrying names.

**What it does not establish.** What carrying is. It constrains the answer.

## Disentanglement meets the same wall

*Bears on: O2.*

**Checked.** Locatello et al., *Challenging Common Assumptions in the
Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations*, ICML 2019. The
impossibility result: without inductive biases on **both the models and the
data**, infinitely many generative models explain a dataset equally well while
differing in their entanglement properties, so the true factors of variation
cannot be identified from observational data alone. Best paper, ICML 2019;
accompanied by a study of over 12,000 trained models.
<https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/locatello19a.html>

In this vocabulary: **cross-cutting relations over a collection do not determine
which are carried.** That is O2, proven unanswerable in a special case.

**Not checked, and a separate claim.** That what breaks the impossibility is
interventional or temporal structure — data in which one factor can be varied
while another is held, or in which the generating process is observed moving.
Locatello's own conclusion is narrower: that inductive bias and implicit
supervision must be made explicit. The interventional route belongs to
subsequent literature which has not been read here. The two must not be run
together, because the first is checked and the second is not.

If the second does hold, it converges on what `specs/ARTICULATION.md` already demands:
occurrences in a *changing*, not items in a set. The demand for a changing would
then be doing more work than it looks like — the same constraint reached
independently elsewhere, for the same reason. It would also fit the regress note
above, since what such methods supply is a property of a generating process
relative to a factorization, not a further relation among items.

**What it does not establish.** Anything about carrying. Three claimed closures
of this gap have been wrong. Treat as a lead.

## A part is a persistence

*Bears on: vocabulary; possibly O2.*

*A is a part of B* when A's sameness persists through B's changing. Nobody has
to be watching. A part is then not a piece a whole is made from but a
persistence its changing sustains, which is consistent with the whole being
prior to its distinctions.

Two consequences. Both continuities yield parts — a wave is a part of the water
exactly as a parcel is — which correctly refuses the intuition that the
constituent is the more genuine one. And because of that, *part* stops meaning
constituent, so adding the word to the theory would buy a term needing immediate
correction and no claim we lack. The insight is worth keeping; the vocabulary is
not.

**What it does not establish.** Carrying. Define parthood this way and arbitrary
sortings become parts too, so the gap survives intact inside the new definition.
Parthood and carrying appear to be one problem in two vocabularies.

## Composition holds only within an articulation

*Bears on: consequences of the kernel. Proposed, under review.*

The first result derived *from* the kernel rather than a verdict on a case.

**Claim.** Where `A < X > B` holds, the identity-fixing reading of "B consists of
As" has no single referent for the persisting B. It refers only to B's footprint
at an instant.

**Derivation.** "Consists of" in the identity-fixing sense asserts that
A-sameness, together with arrangement, refines B-sameness: fix the As and their
arrangement and you have fixed which B. Cross-cutting says neither continuity
refines the other. So the pair cannot stand in that relation.

**Concretely.** Cells are neither necessary nor sufficient for the glider.
Replace every cell and the same glider persists; take the same cells otherwise
arranged, or occupied at another time, and there is no glider.

**What survives.** The glider's footprint at *t* consists of cells, and that is
correct. A frozen frame holds no glider at all — only a configuration. The
equivocation between a persisting thing and its footprint is what makes the
composition claim feel true.

**The four-dimensionalist reply.** If the glider is the sum of its stages, each
made of cells, then it consists of cell-stages. Answer: which stages belong to
the sum is fixed by the glider criterion, not by the cells, so the composition
is parasitic on the identity it claims to build. Note this cuts symmetrically —
which stages belong to *this parcel* is equally fixed by the parcel criterion.
It shows 4D composition is criterion-relative, not that it is wrong.

**Recursive in the body.** Matter turns over, so same body and different matter;
matter passes on, so same matter and different body. Organs are themselves
persisting forms through turning-over cells. So "the body consists of organs" is
footprint-true and persistence-empty at every level.

**Consequence.** Composition is a relation *within* one articulation, among the
many that appear once a limited criterion of identity is adopted. It is never a
relation *across* an articulated pair. "Consists of" works only after a cut.

**Stated in quotients.** Each continuity induces a quotient of the occurrences.
Quotient by cell-sameness and the elements are cell-histories; quotient by
glider-sameness and the elements are gliders, and no cells appear there at all.
So *consists of* is a relation among elements of one quotient. There is no
composition across quotients, because the terms are not there to relate. "Works
only after a cut" is "works only within a quotient" — the same claim in what the
kernel already provides.

This also says what the glider is without reaching for abstraction. It is not a
relation among cells with the particular cells left out: that would be a
coarsening of the cell quotient, and a coarsening nests rather than cross-cuts.
Two gliders on one board share an arrangement and are not the same glider, so
glider-sameness is not arrangement-sameness. The glider is a particular traced
through the changing, and it is an element of the other quotient.

**Guard: not orthogonal dimensions.** The two quotients are quotients *of one
set*. Cross-cutting quantifies over occurrences shared by both relations — `x`
and `y` are the same objects in both clauses of the test — so separate domains
would leave nothing to quantify over and make the condition vacuous. Two
dimensions give two unrelated structures, not an articulated pair.

Worse, orthogonality is the signature of the case the theory refuses.
Independent factors are exactly what colour and shape are, and that pair is
uncarried. Parcel and wave are not independent; they are bound together by the
water's changing, which is what carrying was introduced to name. Independence
fights carrying rather than expressing it.

*Dimension* is also a word the theory removed. Spatial metaphors reinstate
stacking; that is how horizontal, vertical and deep became levels.

**Corrections applied to the argument as first stated.**

*Refinement is by arrangement too.* "Consists of" asserts that A-sameness
together with arrangement refines B-sameness, not A-sameness alone. The
contradiction is therefore not immediate, and the claim to defeat is specifically
that A-*identity* constitutes B-*identity*.

*Determination is not constitution.* Cell-level facts fully determine the glider
— that is derivability, which R2 and `examples/glider.md` affirm. An argument
phrased as *the cells do not fix the glider* is false. Phrased as *cell-sameness
does not constitute glider-sameness*, it holds. The "not sufficient" step must be
read as constitution, never as determination.

*Not false — without referent.* The persisting glider has no fixed set of cells,
so the identity-fixing claim has nothing to refer to. Type-level talk — gliders
are made of cells — is untouched.

**What it does not establish.** Nothing where co-articulation does not hold; it inherits open
item 2, and for an uncarried pair "consists of" might fail for unrelated
reasons. It does not privilege the formation side: *the cell consists of
glider-stages* is false by the same argument. And it says nothing about
causation — the heart really does pressurize blood.

- **Latent in the composition argument above**, where "the cells are not

non-derivability. It does not, and R2 says so outright. Expect
to reach for it anyway; check every occurrence.

## Field: the glider case in physics, with a risk

*Bears on: examples. A sketch, not run.*

A site can persist while the excitations through it change. An excitation can
persist while its sites change.

    site < field > excitation

If this holds, a particle is on the excitation side, so it is not made of field
sites, and "consists of" refers only to its footprint (R4).

**Risk.** Identical quantum particles are indistinguishable. Whether there is a
"same electron" through time is contested, and the formalism may supply only
occupation numbers. If so, excitation-sameness may not be a continuity at all
(D1 needs a particular to trace), and the case is mistyped rather than holding.
Not to be used as an example until this is settled.

## Name

*Bears on: how the theory is described.*

*Identity in Changing Wholes*. Each word is something the kernel requires:
identity is the question (articulation, D9, is the answer); changing, because
sameness is traced over the occurrences of a changing (P2, P3); wholes, because
every claim names its whole (P1, D11).

Refused: *articulation theory* (an established cultural-studies programme, in
which articulation is a link someone makes: the reading misreading #4
refuses); *self-articulation* (reads as a person's self, and makes the whole an
agent, which D9 denies); *theory of wholes* (Husserl's theory of wholes and
parts, whose composition this theory denies across articulations, R4).

## Closure is closed under disjoint union (16 Nov)

Found by an outside reviewer, reproduced in the harness: two distant gliders
traced as one persistence pass checks 1–3 and hold under every implemented
candidate at every step. Each glider produces its own next step, so the pair
does too. The register had predicted the opposite for the trout-turkey; that
prediction is withdrawn. The owner's target stays unset.

Three properties have been run together: persistence (the changing sustains
it), prediction (it forecasts as well as the full state) and control (acting
on it changes an outcome reliably). The union has the first two and is still a
bundle. What a glider has and the union lacks is integration: the glider
cannot be split into parts that each keep going alone.

Proposed for the owner: carried means closed and not splittable into parts
that are each closed (indecomposability; compare the organismal/colonial line
in Krakauer et al. on individuality, unverified). Before it is adopted, three
probes are registered so it cannot be a patch: two gliders that interact once,
a glider meeting an eater, the rule-184 jam.

Also found: closure carries 1 of the 127 cells a glider crosses, so
`cell < grid > glider` cannot hold while D11 needs both sides carried by
closure. Proposed: elemental continuities are carried by the form of the rule
(each cell is updated from its own neighbourhood); pattern continuities by
closure and integration. Owner decision.

## Prior-art check (17 Nov)

- Krakauer et al. (2020), checked against the paper's abstract: individuals
  are aggregates that propagate information from their past into their
