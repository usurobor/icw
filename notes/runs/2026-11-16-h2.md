# H2 run: articulation versus production

Tests H2 in [HYPOTHESES.md](../HYPOTHESES.md). Each claim names a cause and an
effect as sets of occurrences (step, row, column) in one Game of Life history,
and a label fixed by construction: *production* (the effect depends on the
cause), *none* (it does not), or *articulation* (cause and effect are the same
occurrences read under two criteria).

## Candidates

- **Reach.** Identical sets: articulation. Disjoint sets: production when every
  effect occurrence lies within reach of some earlier cause occurrence (board
  distance no more than the steps between them), otherwise none.
- **Intervention.** Identical sets: articulation, since acting on one is acting
  on the other. Disjoint sets: delete the cause's live cells at its first step,
  rerun, and call it production when any effect occurrence changes, otherwise
  none.

Both share the identical-set clause, so the first claim tests that clause and
the rest test how each candidate separates production from none.

## Claims and labels

| # | Claim | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The glider's cells produce the glider (same occurrences, two criteria) | articulation |
| 2 | The glider at step 0 produces the glider at step 40 | production |
| 3 | A gun produces the first glider it emits | production |
| 4 | A glider striking a block produces the debris | production |
| 5 | Glider A produces glider B through a one-time turner (indirect splice) | production |
| 6 | Glider A at step 0 produces distant glider B at step 20 (splice, out of reach) | none |
| 7 | Glider A at step 0 produces distant glider B at step 100 (splice, within reach) | none |
| 8 | One glider from a gun produces the next one (common cause) | none |

## Predictions, committed before the harness runs

- **Intervention:** all eight correct.
- **Reach:** 1–6 correct; 7 and 8 wrong (reported as production). Reach marks
  where influence is possible, and a distant glider or a sibling from the same
  gun is within reach without depending on the cause.

If both predictions hold, the first candidate in H2 needs dependence added to
reach, and "linked by reach" becomes "dependent, hence within reach".
