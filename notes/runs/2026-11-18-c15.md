# C15: C14 with one notion of interacting

Candidate C15 (`notes/CASES.md`). Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c15.py`.

Why. In C14 two groups count as joined in history only through one-step
dependence of live cells, while integration detects interaction by the
contrast, which also sees cells that stay dead. The pulsar's pieces interact
only through such cells, so C14 judged its last step over the tracing's own
one-step window and split it (`notes/runs/2026-11-18-c14.md`). One notion of
interacting should serve both. Also two code faults: rule 184's runs are cut
at the ring's seam (iteration 1), fixed by computing runs on the ring.

## Declared before any C15 code ran

C14 unchanged except:

1. **History.** The tracing's history graph joins pieces by one-step
   dependence (as C14) and, at each step, joins the pieces of each
   interaction component found there (pieces linked by nonzero pairwise
   contrast over the window used at that step). Steps are processed in order,
   so a step's windows depend only on joins at earlier steps and at that step's
   descent.
2. **Code.** Rule 184 runs wrap around the ring.

Everything is rerun: grid, shift and representation checks, registered
probes, the six boards of the C14 run, the hunter and new cases, and the
rule-5 iteration (noise; rule 184).
