# C14: C13 with idle exhaust and history-dependent windows

Candidate C14 (`notes/CASES.md`). A revision of C13 after the hunter round
(`notes/runs/2026-11-18-c13-hunt.md`). Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c14.py`.

Why. Two findings drive it. (1) The lightweight spaceship and the three
registered fragment misses share one cause: a cell that the previous stage
produced and that changes nothing afterwards (*idle exhaust*) makes the stage
tie with the stage without it. (2) The pulsar fails at its last step because
C10's "finish three cells apart" rule reads a lull the run cuts short as an
end. The 16 Nov by-product ruling says fragments that have become independent
are not constituents and "may be continuities of their own". C14 reads
*independent* as: has lasting effects of its own. Idle exhaust has none. This
reading is the orchestrator's; the owner rules on it.

## Declared before any C14 code ran

C13's clauses, with three changes.

1. **Idle exhaust.** A piece of a stage F_t is *produced* when setting F_{t−1}'s
   live cells to 0 at t−1 changes one of its cells at t, and *idle* when
   setting its own cells to 0 at t changes no cell at any later step of the
   run. Closeness does not count F_t minus its idle produced pieces as a
   different stage from F_t (a rival equal to it is not a rival); likewise for
   the continuation F_{t+1}. Nothing at the tracing's first step is produced.
2. **Splits.** A split of F_t is two groups of pieces, each with lasting
   effects (dependents at the window's last step), with zero contrast over the
   window. The window depends on the groups' history: if no piece of one group
   and no piece of the other are joined in the tracing's one-step dependence
   graph up to t (C13c's graph), the window is the tracing's own, (t, t₁], and
   a split there is a split. If they are joined, the window is the rest of the
   run, (t, T]; a zero contrast there is a lull the run cannot settle, and the
   step is *unresolved*. C10's three-cell rule is dropped.
3. **Wrap.** Isolated evolution and pieces are computed on the wrapping board
   (code fault H-11).

Clause E unchanged. No threshold is fitted.

`c14.py`):

  short window applies at step 119, one step, and the split stands. The
  history graph and the contrast use two different notions of interacting.

## What this shows

1. **C14 matches the whole frozen register:** 11 of 11 grid rows, 10 of 10
   probe parts, both elemental sides, shift and representation checks. It is
   the first candidate to do so.
2. **It does so by one reading of the by-product ruling:** a fragment is
   independent when it has lasting effects of its own; idle exhaust stays in
   the stage that produced it. Under the other reading (every independent
   fragment leaves), C13a is the consistent variant and the three frozen
   parts are misses. The fork is one owner line.
3. **Three hunter cases remain against it, each pending an owner target:**
   the spaceship at a tracing's first step (exhaust cannot be told from an
   unrelated idle cell without a previous stage); the pulsar's last step
   (two notions of interacting disagree); and every multi-piece tracing at the
   run's end, where the window ruling makes a lull unresolved.
4. **The interval of the probes and the candidate's integration can
   disagree** where products sit within two cells without affecting each
   other (U-gliders 5).
