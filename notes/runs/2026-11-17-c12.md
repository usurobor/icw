# C12: outflow share

Candidate C12 (`notes/CASES.md`). The forward half of C11. C11 asked where a
stage's information comes from. C12 asks where the previous stage's
information goes: how much of it reaches the next stage of the same
continuity, and how much flows to cells outside it. Source: ICW, after C11;
the forward half of Krakauer et al. (2020). Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c12.py`.

## Declared before any C12 code ran

### Variables

For a step t → t+1 of a tracing, with footprints F_t and F_{t+1}:

- **Previous stage O:** the states at t of the cells of F_t.
- **Receivers R_t:** the cells at t+1 that have at least one input in F_t.
  Life: the 3 × 3 neighbourhood of each cell of F_t. Rule 184: the cell and
  its two neighbours.
- **Next stage:** the receivers that belong to F_{t+1}.
- **Outside:** the receivers not in F_{t+1}, live or dead at t+1.

A cell of F_{t+1} outside R_t has no input in F_t and receives nothing from it.

### Ensemble and per-cell term

The C11 ensembles, unchanged. Knockout (primary): each live input cell
independently kept or set to 0, probability 1/2 each. Flip (secondary): every
input independently kept or flipped. Each receiver c has at most 9 inputs, so
the ensemble is enumerated exactly.

For each receiver c, with O_c its inputs in F_t and S_c its other inputs:

    own_c = I(Y_c ; O_c | S_c) = H(Y_c | S_c)

the same term C11 calls *own*: what c's next state gets from the previous
stage, given everything else c reads.

### Score

    out(t) = Σ_{c ∈ R_t ∩ F_{t+1}} own_c / Σ_{c ∈ R_t} own_c

The numerator is C11's numerator. C11 divides it by what the next stage
receives from all sources. C12 divides it by what the previous stage sends to
all receivers. Summing over cells ignores dependence between receivers, as in
C11.

A step whose denominator is 0 (the previous stage sends no information) is
not judged and is counted.

**Score of a tracing:** the minimum of out(t) over judged steps. **A step is
carried** when out(t) is above θ. Verdicts go through the existing harness
(`tools/gol/run.py`: trim, checks 1–3, then the step flags; collision scored
there).

### Threshold

θ is the midpoint between the glider's score (anchor+) and the splice's score
(anchor−), computed first and written into this file before any other case is
scored. Sensitivity: every verdict again at 0.8 θ and 1.2 θ. The flip ensemble
gets its own θ, fixed the same way, and is reported only.

### Rules 9 and 13

The rule enters only through C11's lookup tables, built by calling the
transition function. No step number is read. Checked by run: board shifted 7
rows and 13 columns; tracing shifted 7 steps in time.

## Guards (rule 8)

- **Share refused,** iteration-0 grid (11 rows): C12 refuses 6 (splice,
  step-indexed, cyclic, alternating, both collisions; the debris build at
  step 119, away from the crash). Closure refused 7, C11 4.
- **Length:** 34 words. "A step is carried when, with each live input
  independently kept or set to 0, more than θ of the information the previous
  footprint sends to the cells it reaches lands in the next footprint."
- **Predictions:** 3 of 7 right. Registered probes: 8 of 10 parts match.

## What this shows

1. **Outflow and inflow refuse the same jumps.** Every splice scores 0 at the
   switch under both. The jumps were never the hard part.
2. **Outflow sees a form dying into empty cells.** The annihilating collision
   breaks at the crash: the stage's information goes to cells that end dead,
   which lie outside every footprint. C11 missed this; closure caught it.
3. **Outflow is blind to a form ending inside a product.** In the debris
   collision and the eater's merge, the glider's information lands in the
   product, so the tracing that continues into it holds. The eater's glider
   end, which C11 got, is lost.
4. **The indirect splice sits on the threshold.** 0.318 against 0.315; at
   1.2 θ it is refused. Its lowest step is inside the reaction, not at the
   join to B. Under flip the lowest step is the join.
5. **The car holds by the threshold, not by the measure.** Blocked steps give
   0.333 under C11 and C12 alike. The glider sends 37% of its information to
   cells outside its next stage at its weakest phase, so the anchor-fitted θ is low enough to
   pass the car.
6. **Unions pass.** Each glider keeps its own share, so the pair does.
