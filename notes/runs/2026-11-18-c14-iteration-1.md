## Declared before any code for this run

### Noise in the Game of Life

Board 128 × 128, wrapping, 120 steps. After each step of the rule, every cell
flips independently with probability ε. ε = 10⁻⁴ (about 1.6 flips a step) and
10⁻³ (about 16). Seeds 0, 1, 2 (numpy default_rng). The flips belong to the
history: every rerun with cells set to 0 replays the same flips at the same
steps. Isolated evolution, for closeness, is the rule alone.

Two gliders at (10, 10) and (10, 60), as in the grid. Each is tracked as long
as it stays glider-shaped (`track_glider`); its tracing ends there. Cases,
targets transferred from the grid rows (listed under "Needs owner target"):

- Glider A (holds over its tracked span).
- Splice: A to step s − 1, then B, with s the smaller of 100 and the last step
  both are tracked (uncarried).
- Union: A and B as one over the steps both are tracked (uncarried).
- Alternating splice over the same steps (uncarried).

### Moving elements: rule 184

Rings of 240, 120 steps. Densities 0.4, 0.55, 0.7; seeds 0, 1, 2; and the same
rings with noise ε = 10⁻³ per site per step. Cases:

- Every car, followed by the rule's motion (forward when the site ahead is
  empty) from step 0 until noise removes it (target: carried, from the
  traffic jam row).
- Every jam: each run of four or more cars at step 20, followed as the
  longest run of three or more next to the previous one, until there is none
  (target: carried, from the traffic jam row). A jam whose tracing passes
  through a merge or a split is reported separately; no target.
