# C16: window integration by part-dependence (Markov-blanket reading)

Candidate C16 (`notes/CASES.md`). Proposed by the orchestrator at the
owner's request: a Friston-style candidate (Markov blanket, nonequilibrium
steady state) for the bundle group. Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c16.py`.

## The reduction, checked before the run

The orchestrator's analysis: under locality every region of a cellular
automaton has a Markov blanket, its one-cell shell, and so does the union of
two distant gliders (the union of two shells). The blanket alone refuses
nothing (compare Aguilera et al. 2022; Bruineberg et al. 2022). What refuses
the union is that its blanket factors into two blankets whose interiors evolve
apart. A blanket plus minimality reduces to integration over a window, in a
statistical version.

Checked here by argument, and tested in the run:

- Agreed: with a radius-1 rule the shell screens a region's next state from
  everything outside it, for every region, so a blanket exists for any
  tracing whose footprints are regions. The condition is empty as a filter.
- One difference to test. The statistical version judges an ensemble of
  variants of the stage, the actual history only through it. Two parts within
  reach that do not affect each other in the actual history (contrast zero,
  as C13's integration measures) can still be dependent across the ensemble,
  because knocked-out variants of them can interact. The bi-block (two blocks
  one cell apart) is such a case. If its score is positive, the reduction
  holds for parts out of reach and fails for parts within reach: blanket
  minimality is then integration over variants, a different predicate from
  integration in the actual history.

## Declared before any C16 code ran

**Window.** The tracing's first stage F₀ (after trimming), and the window
W = the next min(span, 60) steps of the tracing.

**Splits.** If F₀ has two or more 8-connected pieces: every bipartition of its
pieces (31 drawn with seed 0 if there are more than six pieces). If one piece:
8 bipartitions of its live cells into two non-empty parts, drawn with seed 0.

**Ensemble.** Each live cell of F₀ independently kept or set to 0 with
probability 1/2; everything else as in the history. Enumerated exactly when F₀
has at most 12 live cells, otherwise 2048 draws (seed 0).

**Score.** Y is the states over W of the cells of the tracing's footprints
(each footprint at its own step). For a split (P, Q), with X_P and X_Q the
knockout variables of the two parts, the score is I(X_P ; X_Q | Y) in bits.
X_P and X_Q are independent by construction; the score is 0 when the
future factors into a function of X_P and a function of X_Q, and positive
when it depends on them jointly. The orchestrator's wording, "the parts'
trajectories over W are dependent", needs each future cell assigned to one
part; the window gives no assignment, so the conditional form is used. The
tracing is *integrated* when the minimum score over its splits is above θ.

**Threshold.** θ is the midpoint between the glider's score (anchor+) and the
union's (anchor−), computed first and written here before any other case is
scored. Sensitivity at 0.8 θ and 1.2 θ.

**C16 (combination).** A step is carried when C10's closeness holds (code
unchanged), C12's outflow is above its frozen 0.315, and the tracing is
integrated (one flag for every step). Reported with and without C13's element
clause E.

**Cases.** The grid; the registered probes; bi-block as one; lightweight
spaceship; pulsar; N-1, N-1b, N-2, N-3; Gosper gun followed.

### Amended before any C16 code ran

The conditional score I(X_P ; X_Q | Y) fails by analysis: when the future Y
determines both inputs (a rich, lossless future), it is 0 whether or not the
parts interact, because then I(X_P ; X_Q | Y) = H(X_P|Y) + H(X_Q|Y) −
H(X_P, X_Q|Y) = 0. Replaced by the orchestrator's wording with an explicit
assignment:

- **Regions.** P's region: the window's footprint cells (each at its step)
  whose state varies when X_P varies with Q's cells all kept, or with Q's
  cells all set to 0. Q's region likewise. Y_P and Y_Q are the states of the
  two regions.
- **Score:** I(Y_P ; Y_Q) under the joint ensemble, in bits. Parts whose
  regions are disjoint and each fixed by its own part score 0 exactly; parts
  that share future cells score above 0.
- Region runs: all 2^|P| settings when |P| ≤ 8, else 256 draws (seed 0).

Everything else as declared.

## What this shows

1. **The blanket condition reduces to integration only for parts out of
   reach.** Parts out of each other's reach over the window score 0 exactly
   (union, A and B as one, the glider with a far spark) or near it under
   sampling (two fed eaters, 0.320). Parts within reach that never affect
   each other in the actual history score high: the bi-block (2.296) and the
   two products of an interaction traced from the interval's end (N-1,
   10.996), which C13's contrast in the actual history refuses. Knocked-out
   variants of the parts interact where the parts themselves do not. So
   blanket minimality over an ensemble is integration over variants, a
   different predicate from integration in the actual history. This is the
   counterexample to the reduction as stated: it holds in the far case and
   fails in the near case.
2. **It counts idle cells as parts that factor off.** The spaceship's spark
   changes nothing, so its region is empty and the split scores 0: the
   spaceship is refused at the window level. C14 handles the same cell as
   idle exhaust.
3. **It holds fission bundles** (N-1, the gun with its emitted gliders): a
   judgement at the window's start cannot see parts that separate later.
4. **The combination addresses the three failure groups on the grid** (11 of
   11), as the orchestrator predicted, and inherits C10's ties on the probes.
