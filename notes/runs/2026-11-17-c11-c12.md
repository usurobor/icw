# C11 ∧ C12: inflow and outflow together

A step is carried when it passes C11 (own-past share above 0.5,
`notes/runs/2026-11-17-c11.md`) and C12 (outflow share above 0.315,
`notes/runs/2026-11-17-c12.md`), each at its frozen threshold. No new tuning.
Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c11_c12.py`.

## Declared before the run

- **Flags:** per step, carried when C11's flag and C12's flag are both
  carried. A step that one of them does not judge (denominator 0) is carried
  by that one, as in each run.
- **Ensemble:** knockout decides. Flip reported with each run's flip θ
  (C11 0.224, C12 0.141).
- **Sensitivity:** both thresholds scaled together by 0.8 and by 1.2.
- **Comparison:** the same grid and probe parts under closure, C9, C10 (as
  implemented in `tools/gol/candidates.py` and `tools/gol/probes.py`), C11,
  C12 and the conjunction. Closure on rule 184: the stage's run alone equals
  the next stage.
- **Shift check:** as in C11 and C12.

The conjunction's flags follow from the two runs' flags, so its grid can be
read off them. This run computes it, adds the comparison, and checks the
predictions, which were frozen before Run A's results existed.

## What this shows

1. **On the grid the conjunction equals C12.** Every row C11 refuses, C12
   refuses too. B-1 fails for that reason.
2. **On the probes the two halves cover each other.** C11 refuses the
   glider's continuation into the eater's merged piece; C12 refuses the
   annihilating collision. The conjunction keeps both and keeps the eater's
   persistence. It loses the car, which only C12 held.
3. **The remaining misses fall in three groups.** Bundles: the union and A
   and B as one. Ending into a product: the debris collision and the indirect
   splice, where the followed tracing passes into the reaction and on. The
   car, which every rule-reading candidate but C12 refuses, and C12 only by
   0.018.
4. **C10 and the conjunction fail on disjoint parts.** C10 misses the
   interaction probes, the eater's persistence and the car; the conjunction
   misses the union, the indirect splice, the debris collision, A and B as
   one, and the car. C10's integration refuses bundles, and its
   comparison with the stage run alone refuses products. The flows handle feeding and merging.
