# Integration probes

Candidate C9: closure plus integration (`specs/ARTICULATION.md`, D8). Targets
frozen by the owner in `notes/CASES.md` before this run. Rerun:
`python3 tools/gol/probes.py`.

## Predictions

Committed before any code for integration or the probes was written.

### Integration as computed

Atoms are the 8-connected blobs of a stage. The stage is integrated unless its
blobs split into two groups that each, run alone for one step, give together
what the whole gives. Blobs three or more cells apart cannot affect each
other, so they always split. A carried step needs closure and an integrated
stage. An empty tracing is not a continuity; tracings are trimmed to their
first and last live frames.

### Intervals, fixed before the run

- **Start:** the first step at which the two patterns, each run alone, come
  within two cells of each other.
- **End:** the first step from which the actual products equal their
  separate pieces each run alone, to the end of the run.

### Against the predictions

- Matched: the whole iteration-0 grid; the union refused; both gliders and
  the glider-into-eater carried until contact; the two gliders as one refused;
  the eater's persistence refused; the jam and the passing car refused.
- Missed: the interaction, in both probes. Predicted carried over the interval,
  with the risk named. Closure holds at every step of both interactions; every
  break is integration's.

## Findings

1. **C9 is not O2.** Integration refuses the union, the case it was built
   for, and fails the two probes registered to test it. By the owner's
   criterion, integration as computed here is a patch.
2. **Integration has a time scale.** In both interactions the parts, at some
   steps, evolve apart exactly as they do together, then act on each other
   again a step or two later. Judged one step at a time, the interaction
   flickers between one whole and two. "Affecting each other" holds over an
   interval, not at an instant: the interval was fixed that way, and the
   candidate judged single steps.
3. **Closure fails whatever persists through what flows in or acts on it.**
   The eater persists across contact in fact, and C9 breaks it. The jam keeps
   its length while every car passes through it, and C9 carries it on no step,
   because a jam run alone sheds its front car and gains none. A car is
   refused on each step it is blocked. All three, as predicted.
4. **Both failures are the same question:** carrying judged over a window,
   and to a degree. Integration needs a window; closure needs "mainly", since
   the jam's next stage depends on its own previous stage plus one incoming
   car. The noise iteration was to introduce "mainly"; the jam and the eater
   require it without noise.
