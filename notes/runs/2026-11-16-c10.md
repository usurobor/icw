# C10: closest continuer, integration over the interval

Owner rulings of 16 Nov (`specs/ARTICULATION.md`, D8). Registered cases frozen.
Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/run.py` and `python3 tools/gol/probes.py`.

## Declared before any C10 code

### Closeness

How close a later stage X is to an earlier stage F: the overlap (Jaccard)
between X and what F alone produces in one step under the rule. Closure is the
special case of closeness 1.

### Comparison set

For the step F_t → F_{t+1}:
- **Continuations of F_t:** the proposed F_{t+1}; each 8-connected piece of
  the board at t+1 within one cell of F_t; the empty stage.
- **Predecessors of F_{t+1}:** the proposed F_t; each piece of the board at t
  within one cell of F_{t+1}; the empty stage.

Single pieces only, no unions of pieces: a union would always be the closest
predecessor of a merger, so nothing could persist through one.

### Candidates

- **C10, best continuer.** A step is carried when F_{t+1} is the unique
  closest continuation of F_t, F_t is the unique closest predecessor of
  F_{t+1}, and the stage is integrated. Ties carry nothing.
- **C10s, share.** Closeness above 0.5 in both directions, and integrated.
- **Integration over the interval.** A stage is one whole unless its pieces
  split into two groups that, each run alone from that step, reproduce the
  tracing to the end of the run and finish more than two cells apart. If they
  reproduce it but finish within two cells, the step is *unresolved*.

### Tracings fixed for the new parts

- **The eater persists:** the eater's piece, followed: at each step, the
  8-connected pieces that hold the eater's cells or grew from them.
- 1D rule 184: pieces are runs of consecutive cars.

The car fails both because a blocked car, alone, would move: its own next
stage never comes from it. Whether a car is carried may depend on how the rule
is presented. Written as "each site from its neighbours", cars have no
identity; written as "each car hops if the site ahead is empty", they do. The
same rule, two presentations, two verdicts on the car under a reading of
carrying off the rule. Owner question.

Known objection, sent to audit: under a best continuer, whether A continues
into B can depend on what else is within reach (the "only x and y" principle).

## Audit (`notes/audits/2026-11-16-best-continuer.md`): changes applied

- "Best continuer" is this register's term. The parallel is Nozick's
  *closest continuer* (Philosophical Explanations, 1981, ch. 1), claimed only
  as a parallel. Overlap with isolated production is one dimension of his
  closeness, chosen here; "in the spirit of", not his measure.
- C10 breaks the only-x-and-y principle by design (Williams, as named in
  Noonan, *Personal Identity*, §1.11, p. 13): a rival piece within reach can
  cancel a continuation. Nozick accepts this knowingly.
- C10s is a raw overlap threshold applied to the proposal only; rivals are not
  consulted, so two continuations can both pass.
- Overlaps are exact fractions; a tie is logged as a tie, apart from
  uncarried. Rerun after the change: every mark identical, no ties.
- Integration over the rest of the run is C9's integration with a horizon, a
  patch under test, not a principled part of the candidate.

### Against the predictions

- Matched: the iteration-0 grid for C10; the union; both gliders and the
  glider into the eater, until contact; the glider refused as continuing into
  the merged piece; the jam now holds under both; the car fails under both.
- Missed, C10s: the collision (the glider continues into glider-plus-block at
  overlap 5/6) and the eater's merger (overlap exactly 1/2, not above it).
  Both are the threshold's arbitrariness, as the audit warned.
- Missed, both: the interaction in both probes, and the eater's persistence.
  Closeness is never the cause. At every such step a fragment of the reaction
  (one to four cells) dies or drifts off without affecting the rest. By the
  owner's window ruling, a part that runs apart for the rest of the run is not
  part of the whole, so the tracing that keeps the fragment is not one whole
  at that step.

## Findings

1. **Graded closeness fixes the jam.** Its next stage is 19 of 21 cells its
   own, and no rival is closer. The fed-structure failure of closure is gone.
2. **The share threshold fails where the closest continuer does not:** the
   collision and the eater's merger. Two cases for "best" over "share".
3. **The remaining failures are by-products.** Fragments that die or drift
   off without effect sit inside every reaction tracing. Whether such a
   fragment belongs to the whole while it lasts is an owner question. A
   diagnostic run (not scored) that refuses to split off a part that dies made
   things worse: such parts left steps unresolved, and the eater still broke.
4. **The car fails under every candidate.** A blocked car, alone, would move,
   so its next stage never comes from it. Whether rule 184 carries cars may
   depend on presenting the rule as sites updated from neighbours, or as cars
   hopping when the site ahead is empty.

## Status

C10 is not O2. It passes everything frozen except the interaction probes, the
eater's persistence and the car. The first two turn on one owner question
(by-products); the third on another (the rule's presentation).
