# C10: best continuer, integration over the interval

Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/run.py` and `python3 tools/gol/probes.py`.

## Declared before any C10 code

### Closeness

How close a later stage X is to an earlier stage F: the overlap (Jaccard)
between X and what F alone produces in one step under the rule. Closure is the
special case of closeness 1.

### Comparison set

For the step F_t → F_{t+1}:
- **Continuations of F_t:** the proposed F_{t+1}; each 8-connected piece of
  the board at t+1 within one cell of F_t; the empty stage.
- **Predecessors of F_{t+1}:** the proposed F_t; each piece of the board at t
  within one cell of F_{t+1}; the empty stage.

Single pieces only, no unions of pieces: a union would always be the closest
predecessor of a merger, so nothing could persist through one.

### Candidates

- **C10, best continuer.** A step is carried when F_{t+1} is the unique
  closest continuation of F_t, F_t is the unique closest predecessor of
  F_{t+1}, and the stage is integrated. Ties carry nothing.
- **C10s, share.** Closeness above 0.5 in both directions, and integrated.
- **Integration over the interval.** A stage is one whole unless its pieces
  split into two groups that, each run alone from that step, reproduce the
  tracing to the end of the run and finish more than two cells apart. If they
  reproduce it but finish within two cells, the step is *unresolved*.

### Tracings fixed for the new parts

- **The eater persists:** the eater's piece, followed: at each step, the
  8-connected pieces that hold the eater's cells or grew from them.
- 1D rule 184: pieces are runs of consecutive cars.
