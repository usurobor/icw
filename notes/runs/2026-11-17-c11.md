# C11: own-past share

Candidate C11 (`notes/CASES.md`). Source: Krakauer, Bertschinger, Olbrich,
Flack and Ay (2020), "The information theory of individuality", *Theory in
Biosciences* 139: individuals are aggregates that propagate information from
their past into their futures. ICW reading: a stage is carried when it gets
more of its information from the persistence's own previous stage than from
its surroundings. Graded. Rerun: `python3 tools/gol/c11.py`.

## Declared before any C11 code ran

### Variables

For a step t → t+1 of a tracing, with footprints F_t and F_{t+1} (D3, sets of
occurrences):

- **Next stage Y:** the states at t+1 of the cells of F_{t+1}.
- **Inputs:** the cells at t within the rule's reach of F_{t+1}. Life: the
  3 × 3 neighbourhood of each cell. Rule 184: the cell and its two
  neighbours.
- **Own past O:** the inputs that belong to F_t.
- **Surroundings S:** the inputs outside F_t.

Cells of F_t outside the reach of F_{t+1} cannot affect Y and play no part.

### Ensemble (primary: knockout)

Each live input cell independently keeps its state or is set to 0, with
probability 1/2 each. Dead inputs stay dead. Own and surrounding cells vary
independently. 0 is the rule's quiescent state: an all-0 neighbourhood gives
0, in Life and in rule 184. So the surroundings supply what is actually there,
and empty surroundings supply nothing.

### Score

Each cell c of F_{t+1} depends on at most 9 inputs, so the ensemble is
enumerated exactly: at most 512 configurations per cell. No sampling, so no
sample size and no estimation error.

- own_c = H(Y_c | S_c) = I(Y_c ; O_c | S_c): what c's next state gets from the
  own past, given the surroundings.
- sur_c = H(Y_c | O_c) = I(Y_c ; S_c | O_c): what it gets from the
  surroundings, given the own past.

The equalities hold because O_c and S_c fix Y_c. These are the two
conditional terms of a two-source split of the information in Y_c. They are
this register's reading of the paper's split, not checked against its
formulas.

    share(t) = Σ_c own_c / Σ_c (own_c + sur_c)

A step whose denominator is 0 (neither source carries any information) is not
judged and is counted. Summing over cells ignores dependence between the cells
of Y: a proxy, chosen because it can be computed exactly.

**Score of a tracing:** the minimum share over its judged steps. **A step is
carried** when its share is above θ. Verdicts go through the existing harness
(`tools/gol/run.py`: trim, checks 1–3 against *same cell*, then the step
flags; collision scored as there).

### Threshold

θ is the midpoint between the glider's score (anchor+) and the splice's score
(anchor−). Both are computed first, θ is written into this file, and only
then is any other case scored. Sensitivity: every verdict again at 0.8 θ and
1.2 θ.

### Secondary ensemble (flip)

The same score with every input cell, live or dead, independently kept or
flipped with probability 1/2: uniform resampling. Its own θ, fixed the same
way from the same anchors. Reported to show whether the verdicts depend on the
ensemble. It does not decide C11's verdicts.

### Rules 9 and 13

The rule enters only through a lookup table built by calling `life.step` (or
`step184`) on a zero-padded patch for each neighbourhood. No step number is
read and every quantity is computed from relative positions. Checked by run:
the board shifted 7 rows and 13 columns, and the tracing shifted 7 steps in
time (7 empty frames before the history).

### Cases

Every script row of the seed list the harness can build, targets as frozen:

- From `tools/gol/cases.py`: glider; two identical gliders at once; splice;
  indirect splice; both collision builds; step-indexed; cyclic splice (first
  build); alternating splice; union of two distant gliders.
- From `tools/gol/probes.py`: two gliders that interact; glider meets an
  eater; rule-184 jam and car. The rule-184 build also stands for the traffic
  jam row (anchor+, same car / same jam: both carried).
- Trivial groupings: checks 1–3 as in run.py; C11 also scored on the whole
  board directly.
- Built for this run: **two gliders, one arrangement** (glider B on glider A's
  lane, 20 cells behind; B crosses A's starting sites 80 steps later in the
  same phase; each traced; target holds), and **incars** (the glider while it
  is inside a 40 × 40 square it crosses; pair same glider / same incar; target
  nested at check 3).
- Diagnostic, no row: each of the cells the glider crosses, traced as one site
  over the whole run (elemental continuity).
- Trout-turkey has the union's build and no target: score reported only.

Not built: the flame row's script variant (a structure fed by a glider
stream), Tibbles and Tib, the problem of the many, fission, the
teletransporter (parked). Panel rows are skipped.

## Predictions

Frozen, the orchestrator's. Numbered 1–9. Each is marked right or wrong after
the run and not edited.

1. Glider: high own-past share; holds.
2. Splice: share drops at the switch step; refused (uncarried).
3. Alternating splice: low; refused.
4. Union of two distant gliders: high; C11 fails to refuse it (same failure as
   closure). Integration would still be needed.
5. Step-indexed grouping (glider before t0, block after): drop at t0;
   refused.
6. Two gliders that interact, and glider meets eater: own-past share falls
   during the interaction interval.
7. Whole-board trivial grouping: share 1 trivially (no surroundings); C11
   does not refuse it (check 3 does).
8. Single cell (elemental continuity): low share, because a cell's next state
   is driven by its neighbours. Consistent with closure carrying 1 of 127
   cells; supports the proposal that elemental continuities are carried by the
   form of the rule instead.
9. Rule-184 jam: scores lower than the glider, because its stages are fed by
   inflowing cars; possibly a miss against its target *holds*. Car:
   intermediate.

How each is judged, fixed now. *High* means above θ, *low* at or below θ.

1. Glider score above θ and verdict holds.
2. The switch step has the splice's lowest share, below its other steps;
   verdict uncarried.
3. Score at or below θ; verdict uncarried.
4. Score above θ; verdict holds.
5. The step from t0 − 1 to t0 has the lowest share; verdict uncarried.
6. In both probes, the minimum share inside the contact interval is below the
   minimum outside it, for the tracings the probes register: the interaction
   tracing (inside against after the interval) and the eater followed as a
   piece (inside against before contact).
7. Whole-board share 1 at every judged step; C11 holds it.
8. Most crossed cells score at or below θ.
9. Jam score below the glider's; car score strictly between the splice's and
   the jam's.
