# Entanglement: one well-understood case, two readings

Parked (CASES). Physics claims are standard; citations unaudited.

## The case

Two qubits in a Bell state, (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2. No pair of single-qubit states
multiplies to it. Each qubit alone is in the same state, maximally mixed, for
all four Bell states; the difference shows only in joint measurements.
Separated and evolved locally, U_A ⊗ U_B, the pair stays entangled with no
interaction. Local statistics never depend on what is done to the distant
qubit, so nothing signals (Nielsen and Chuang 2000, ch. 2).

## Two questions that coincide in the Game of Life

- Does each part's evolution depend on the other? (dynamics)
- Does the whole's state reduce to the parts' states? (state)

A board's state is always its cells' states together, so in the toy domain the
two questions are one, and integration as tested (C9, C10) asks the first. A
Bell pair answers no to both: local dynamics, joint state. D8 has to say which
question integration asks.

## Elemental reading

Start from the two particles and ask what whole they form. Entanglement is then
a bond between parts that persists without interaction: puzzling, because
nothing holds the parts together.

## Pattern reading

Start from the change. The Bell correlation is one continuity with two
footprints. Nothing needs to hold two things together, because there were
never two things at this level. The glider union is the converse: two patterns
whose state splits cleanly. "Does not split" fits integration better than
"interacts".

## A cross-cutting witness

Teleportation (Bennett et al. 1993) moves a qubit's state onto another
particle; each particle stays where it is. Same state, different particle:
roughly `particle < lab > quantum state`, the quantum glider. The no-cloning
theorem (Wootters and Zurek 1982) forbids copying a state, so this pattern
cannot branch (O4). Open at check 4: does the protocol carry the state, or does
the describer name the output "the same"? The protocol needs a measurement and
a classical message.

## Status

Two parked probes: the Bell pair under local evolution; teleportation.
Evaluator: panel, or a quantum cellular automaton later. A classical relative
reaches the toy in the noise iteration: two gliders born in one random
reaction, correlated without interacting.
