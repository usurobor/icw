# Audit 2026-11-18: C13 to C16, and the claim that C15 matches every frozen target

Auditor: a separate session. It produced none of the work audited. Targets
are read as they stood when C15 ran (commit 2c82c97). The owner rulings
recorded afterwards (c9341e7, 1df8c6d) are applied only in the last section.
Rerun the auditor's checks: `python3 tools/gol/audit_c15.py hunt`, `ablate`,
`checks`. Case labels H-21 to H-25 are this audit's own. They continue the
numbering of the C13 hunter round, which used H-1 to H-15.

## Verdict

"C15 matches every frozen target" is partly true. On the frozen tracings the
numbers reproduce exactly: grid 11 of 11, probes 10 of 10. Two clauses written
after the misses carry that match, and each is necessary for it. The
unseen-board test exercises those clauses on one independent part. The noise
and car tests cannot fail by construction. Four of the auditor's five hunter
cases contradict owner targets or rulings already recorded when C15 ran, and
the fifth contradicts a ruling recorded since. C15 is a stack of about twelve
clauses. Its stated form runs to about 132 words. The run reports 92.

## 3. Patches

Four clauses were added after a miss. Each was aimed at named cases.

| Clause | Added | Aimed at |
|---|---|---|
| Idle exhaust: a stage and the stage less its produced idle pieces count as one in closeness | C14 | interaction steps 16 and 27; eater step 49 (two parts); lightweight spaceship |
| Lasting effects: a group with no effect at the window's end does not split the stage | C14 | the same three parts (with the dying-part exemption of C13b as precedent) |
| History-dependent window: joined groups judged to the run's end, then unresolved; C10's three-cell rule dropped | C14 | pulsar (hunter case, no target) |
| History also joined by interaction components | C15 | pulsar |

The run flags only the first as a patch. The ablation (`audit_c15.py ablate`)
shows the second is equally a patch. With either clause off, C15 fails the
same three registered parts (idle off: steps 16, 27, 49; lasting effects off:
also 15, 40, 48). The C15 join changes no frozen verdict: C14 and C15 agree on
all 37 parts.

Is the unseen-board test fair? The boards come from the registered searches,
so the clauses could not shape them. Two facts weaken the test.

1. All six unseen eater boards (C13's 1 to 3, C14's 4 to 6) are exact
   space-time translates of the registered eater board: board k is the
   registered history moved by (k, k) cells and 4k steps
   (`audit_c15.py checks`). Twelve of the 27 unseen parts are a shift check
   of the board the clause was fitted to. The glider and collision boards are
   distinct reactions.
2. The clauses change the verdict on 7 of the 27 unseen parts: the six eater
   parts that are translates, and one interaction on U-gliders 6. Without the
   two clauses C15 scores 19 of 27. The independent evidence for the patches
   is one part on one board. The three collisions pass with or without them.

## 5. Hunter cases

Expected verdicts were written before the run, from the targets and rulings
in `notes/CASES.md` and `notes/FINDINGS.md` at commit 2c82c97. C15 was not
modified.

| ID | Case | Expected, and from what | Predicted | C15 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-21 | A glider and a far 13-cell soup that dies by itself at step 97, never touching the glider (checked), traced as one | uncarried: a bundle, as the union of two distant gliders (owner) | holds | holds, every step |
| H-22 | N-2 with the far junk three cells on a diagonal (dies in two steps) | uncarried, as H-21 | holds | holds |
| H-23 | The registered pair's products as one, traced from step 96, 105, 110 | one verdict whatever the start; window ruling: integration ends when the parts evolve apart for the rest of the run | unresolved from 96, refused later | unresolved from 96; uncarried from 105 and 110 |
| H-24 | A and B as one from step 0, continued into the reaction | uncarried before contact: "two glider continuities before contact" (owner) | carried before contact | holds at steps 0–9 for every tracing end tried (13, 21, 96); the registered part, ending at step 10, is refused |
| H-25 | MWSS and HWSS, every live cell, from step 0 | holds, as the glider; the run itself reads the LWSS this way | refused at step 0 only | refused at step 0; hold from step 1 |

What they show:

- **Far dying junk joins any pattern (H-21, H-22).** At each step the junk is
  either part of an exact continuation or, at its last step, produced idle
  exhaust. Its group has no effect at the window's end, so it never splits
  the stage. Each clause is needed: idle off refuses H-21 at 96 and H-22 at
  1; lasting effects off refuses both from step 0. N-2 is refused only
  because a tracing's first step has no produced pieces. Its refusal is an
  artefact of where the tracing starts.
- **Integration reads the future (H-24).** A stage counts as integrated when
  its parts interact anywhere in the window to the tracing's end. Extend the
  union past contact and the steps before contact hold. The frozen part
  matches only because its tracing stops at step 10.
- **The verdict depends on the tracing's start (H-23).** Groups count as
  joined only through the tracing's own history. The C13 run named this a
  fault of C13c (N-1 against N-1b). C15 has it. Under C15 a split of groups
  joined in history is never refused, so the window ruling's "it ends when
  the parts evolve apart for the rest of the observed run" cannot fire. Owner
  question 2 treats this as open; the recorded ruling already spoke to it.
- **Phase artefact (H-25).** The LWSS fault recurs in every spaceship tried.

## 6. Size

Clauses: kind switch (elemental or pattern); site; token by the flow, with
live sites as tokens; forward closeness, with its comparison set; backward
closeness; ties carry nothing; idle exhaust, with "produced" and "idle"
defined; integration by contrast in context; lasting effects; the
history-dependent window; joining by descent; joining by interaction. Twelve,
plus wrap. The D8 wording in `notes/FINDINGS.md` runs to about 132 words and
leaves "produced", "idle" and "joined" undefined. The run's guard reports
C14's 92-word sentence. C15 is a stack of clauses, and §3 traces each added clause to its cases.
