# Write

*Version 1.0.0. ICW-specific; generalize later.*

## Core principle

Say the thing directly, in the words the reader already uses. Every sentence
states what is the case. Nothing is there for rhythm, emphasis or effect.

## Rules

### 1. State what it is

Write the positive claim. Do not build it by first denying something nobody
said.

- ❌ "ICW does not pick a winner. It asks whether…"
- ❌ "It's not about water, it's about the wave."
- ❌ "Not just a theory, but a test."
- ✅ "ICW treats both as possible answers and asks whether…"

A denial is fine only when a reader is likely to believe the denied thing, and
then once, after the positive claim.

### 2. Use the reader's words

Use the everyday word. Use a technical term only when no plain word is as
exact, and explain it in the same sentence.

- ❌ "Two criteria of identity cross-cut."
- ✅ "Each stays the same while the other changes."

### 3. Drop machine-written patterns

Remove these on sight:

- **Contrast frames:** "not X, but Y", "X doesn't…, it…", "rather than X, Y".
- **Staged reveals:** "The answer?", "Here's the thing:", "The result:".
- **Rhetorical questions** answered in the next sentence. A question the
  reader is left holding is fine.
- **Reflex triplets:** three adjectives or three clauses because three sounds
  complete.
- **Signposts:** "In short", "It's worth noting", "Let's", "Simply put",
  "At its core".
- **Inflated words:** crucial, robust, seamless, powerful, landscape,
  journey, delve, leverage, unlock, profound.
- **Closing restatements:** a final sentence that repeats the paragraph.

### 4. One idea per sentence

Short sentences, each carrying one claim. Split any sentence that needs a
second reading.

## Verify

1. Search the text for "not", "rather", "but", "just" and "only". For each
   hit, ask whether it sets up a contrast frame. Rewrite those that do.
2. Read each sentence alone. If it states nothing checkable, cut it.
3. List every word a non-specialist would look up. Replace it, or explain it
   where it first appears.
