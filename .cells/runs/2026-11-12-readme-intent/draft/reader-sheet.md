# Intent: README

What the front page of this repository must achieve, as its owner defines it:
the reader sheet. Readers never receive the key, `intents/readme-key.md`.

## Reader sheet

You came across this project by chance and have other things to do. You
have no training in philosophy, logic or mathematics. Answer in writing. Look
nothing up. A *unit* is a sentence, list item, table row or heading.

**Step 1.** You see the title and opening only. Hand in A1–A3 first.
- A1. In at most four sentences of your own: what is it about, and what does
  it say? Put down everything you took from it.
- A2. Would you read on? Yes, maybe or no. If yes: the one question you most
  want the rest to answer, and the words of the opening that made you want it.
- A3. Quote each word or phrase of the opening whose meaning you could only
  guess at, each with your guess.

**Step 2.** You are now given the whole README. Read it once, top to bottom.
- A4. Quote every unit you could not follow when you reached it, and every
  word you would have had to look up there, each with your guess.
- A5. Quote every unit, headings aside, that only repeats what an earlier
  unit of the README already said.
- A6. What answer did the README itself, not a link, give to your A2
  question, in one sentence? "It says this cannot be answered yet, because
  ..." is an answer. Write "none" if it gave none.
