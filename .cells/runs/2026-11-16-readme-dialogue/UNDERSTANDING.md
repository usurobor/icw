   under one rule applied all at once. Sequential computation is
   implementation; the grid is the drawing, a graph of nodes with eight
   neighbours is the structure, coordinates are labels.
T2 [accepted] The whole is told two ways: elemental (the same square) and
   pattern (the same glider, lighting different squares). Both are tellings
   of the same snapshots; neither sits inside the other. At an instant, a
   glider shows up as some lit squares (footprint).
T3 [accepted] Read as the squares producing the glider, "the glider emerges
   from the squares" mixes the two tellings: it starts in the elemental one,
   ends in the pattern one, and takes the switch for production.
T4 [accepted, as ICW's proposal] Neither telling is more basic, and neither
   produces the other.
